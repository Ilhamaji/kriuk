# DESIGN SYSTEM — kriuk. (Desktop Tray Audio Utility)

- **Platform & Form Factor:** Desktop System Tray Popover (`380px` width × `680px` height), rounded corners (`16px`) with bottom tray caret pointer.
- **Aesthetic & Vibe:** Warm organic minimalism, calm productivity, privacy-first, tactile soft surfaces, zero heavy drop shadows.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Primary Canvas:** Warm Alabaster (`#FCFBF9`)
  - **Secondary Surface / Cards:** Warm Stone (`#F4F2ED`)
  - **Elevated Active Pill:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle hairline border (`#E6E2DA`)
  - **Status Banner Surface:** Soft Sage Tint (`#EFF4E9`) with Sage Icon Accent (`#5B7A4A`)
  - **Primary Brand Accent:** Burnt Terracotta (`#D8683F`) for active toggles, active tab underline, waveform logo, and selected segment text
  - **Icon Container Tints:** Soft Peach (`#FBECE6`) for Microphone icon; Soft Periwinkle (`#EAEBF8`) for Speaker icon
  - **Primary Text:** Deep Charcoal Ink (`#1C1B1A`)
  - **Secondary / Muted Text:** Warm Slate Gray (`#6E6A65`)
  - **Borders & Dividers:** Warm Hairline (`#E8E4DC`)
- **Typography:** Clean geometric/humanist sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans). Semi-bold headings (`15px–16px`), medium labels (`14px`), regular helper captions (`12px`).
- **Component Style:** Pill-shaped toggle switches (`40px × 22px`), 3-option segmented control track (`8px` radius), `10px` radius dropdown inputs, 1px hairline dividers.
